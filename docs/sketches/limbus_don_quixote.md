# Sketch: Don Quixote - La Manchaland (bloodfiend) grimoire  [ON HOLD - not built]

Original designs inspired by the theme; nothing copied from the game's art/audio.

## Core resource: Bloodfeast (0-10)
- Hits that cause Bleed fill it; shown as a red arc like Julius's stored time.
- Vampire Mode drains it; some skills are stronger / change at high Bloodfeast.
- Bleed: our own custom-drawn damage over time (no vanilla effect) - hurts when the bleeding enemy moves or swings.

## Skills
1. Hardblood Lance - a long crimson lance hardens in your hand; 6-block thrust, applies Bleed.
2. Justice Charge - lance levelled, dash 12 blocks carrying the first enemy, slam stop.
3. Sanguine Crescent - wide sweep throwing a blood crescent that splits into droplets landing as small blood spikes (drawn only).
4. Crimson Pact - drink your own blood: trade 2 hearts for instant Bloodfeast + a short speed burst.
5. Thirst - draw blood from every bleeding enemy within 10 blocks (streams flowing to you): heals you, fills Bloodfeast.
6. Hardblood Barricade - ring of blood spears around you for 4 s; touching it knocks back and bleeds.
7. Vampire Mode (toggle) - glowing red eyes, tattered blood cape, faint red aura outline. Drains Bloodfeast; lifesteal on attacks, longer lance, mist step (double-tap sneak: red mist, reappear 5 blocks ahead). Ends when empty.
8. Ultimate: Grand Finale of La Manchaland (full Bloodfeast) - leap, sky tints crimson, a huge hardblood lance forms overhead; dive and pin the target; every bleeding enemy nearby has its Bleed burst at once as a crown of blood spikes.
9. Hardblood Bow - hold to charge; heavy piercing blood arrow (Bleed); full charge splits into five in a fan; in Vampire Mode arrows home on bleeding enemies.
10. Twin Blood Blades - two curved blood swords, fast 6-hit spinning combo (left/right, crimson trails); last hit a cross-cut that bursts Bleed.
11. Blood Fist Barrage - blood-coated fists, ~1.5 s rapid barrage with afterimage fists; ends with a big blood-knuckle knockback blow. Each hit adds Bleed; Vampire Mode: each hit heals a little.
12. Hardblood Greatsword - one long blood sword, heavy overhead cleave; a wave along the ground rising into blood spikes (drawn only). Full Bloodfeast: blade twice as long, longer wave.

Animations: lance thrust, charge pose, crescent sweep, dive, bow draw/release, dual-blade spin combo, punch barrage, overhead cleave. CC0 sounds only (metallic lance rings, wet blood hits, cape flap).
