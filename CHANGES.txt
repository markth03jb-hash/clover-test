Iron Spell Grimoire v1.62.0 - all changed files since v1.0.0

HOW TO USE: extract this zip into the top folder of your project (the one with build.gradle)
and let it overwrite. Then build as usual.

NEW IN 1.62.0
- The new grimoires are the String Grimoire and the Spatial Grimoire, and both can be equipped like the others.
- Grimoire book: the moving preview is gone - the pages are the same parchment as the floating 3D book, with the
  spell's name, icon and description on the left and all its numbers, unlock level and combos on the right.
- Every combo has its own finisher now (no more reskins) - e.g. Gale Fang raises a real tornado for 2 s and throws
  them high, Marrow Pike bursts a colossal bone spike under them, Dash Cleave rips a fissure of spikes ending in an
  eruption, Barrage Finale drops a giant fireball from the sky, Fortress Lock slams steel walls round each enemy.
- Sky Splitter (Demon Slayer): the same crescent slash as the Demon Dweller's, in pure red.
- Event Horizon leaves a lighter trail (Equinox unchanged).
- Spirit Dive: the wind only streams off you while you move, trailing behind like the air you cut through.
- Luck: his lightning is blue (no more yellow-white), forks more, and the sky flash is blue; Lightning Step always
  goes as far as it can (stepping up and down ledges) and flashes right in front of an enemy you aim at.
- String Grimoire: Binding Threads no longer lags when spammed (thin threads only shed sprites when they appear);
  Thread Cocoon now winds round you and moves with you; Battle Dress wears a dress of thread (skirt, ribbons, a
  crown of thread loops, sparkles) the whole time; Stitching shows bright X stitches, a needle and a ring; the Red
  Thread of Fate turns a great red wheel above you with threads to everyone it protects, races a web of red thread
  over the ground, and snaps red threads out round anyone whose blow it erases.
- Spatial Grimoire: the Waypoint gate lies flat on the ground and is big enough to stand on; Space Deletion cracks
  space in gold round the point before the tear opens, then shatters it outward.
- Timers for everything with a duration, and how many hits / dodges / shield are left for everything that has
  them (Ki Foresight, Hushed Dance, Evasion Gate, Thread Cocoon, Red Thread of Fate...).

NEW IN 1.58.0
- Effect quality setting (config: effect_quality = LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH, default HIGH):
  LOW    35% particles, at most 800 pixel sprites and 300 smoke puffs, no glow pass, 1-tick impact frames, lighter shake
  MEDIUM 65% particles, 1600 sprites, 600 puffs, 3-tick impact frames
  HIGH   everything (2400 sprites, 900 puffs, 4-tick impact frames)
- Grimoire page-turn: every cast flips your floating grimoire open (fast) and riffles three pages over in quick
  succession to the spell, with the sound of the pages - seen by everyone around you.
- Grimoire mana bar: while one of this addon's grimoires is equipped, Iron's mana bar makes way for the grimoire's
  own - framed in its trim colour with its clover at the end, mana in its colour with a sheen running through,
  easing to the new value, numbers above it. Asta's has no mana: a black bar with a flickering blood-red edge.
- The grimoire book: sneak + use while holding a grimoire to open it. One spread per spell in the book's order: on
  the left its name, a moving preview (a copy of you playing the spell's cast animation, its icon and the
  grimoire's colours turning behind) and the mastery level it unlocks at; on the right what it does, its base
  damage and other numbers, mana cost, cooldown and cast type. Spells the grimoire hasn't given you yet are empty
  pages. Turn pages with the arrows, arrow keys / A-D or the mouse wheel. The effect quality switch is at the top.
- Spell Clash: when two players' travelling moves (slashes, crescents, balls, gusts...) meet head-on they clash -
  a flash, rings in both colours, sparks, a shake. The stronger (spell power x the size of the move) breaks through;
  the weaker is torn apart and its damage never lands; within 15% of each other both are cancelled. Anti magic
  always cuts through magic. Both players are told the result.
- Grimoire acquisition - the grimoire tower ceremony: craft a Grimoire Altar (book over gold - lapis block - gold,
  three polished deepslate) and use it. The grimoires rise out of it and circle above, faster and faster, until one
  leaves the ring and comes to your hands: it has chosen you. Which one is weighted by how you play (your stats):
  fighters draw Asta's, Yami's and Mereoleona's; archers and fliers Yuno's; swimmers Nozel's; fire-starters Magna's;
  runners and jumpers Luck's; skeleton hunters Zenon's; shield users Klaus's. Asta's five-leaf grimoire is the
  rarest and favours those with little magic (low experience level).
  In survival you are chosen once and the grimoire is bound to you - nobody else can cast from it. Lose it and the
  altar calls it back. In creative the ceremony can be repeated. The grimoires can no longer be crafted.
- The page-turn now starts the moment you begin casting.

- Zetten: the solid 3D cleave, X blades, fissure and shockwaves are back under the sprites.

  busy - and the last violet is gone (wind-up edge, rift and ring are black-red now).

- Tornado Fang's tornado is back, and Sylph's Breath and Spirit Dive look as they did before: wind keeps its 3D

- The radial wheel: Asta's five-leaf clover now has five broad heart-shaped leaves with thin slits between them.

NEW IN 1.56.0
- No more delay: effects are never held back any more - every effect shows the moment the move sends it, so nothing
  comes out after the animation has finished. Sounds play with the move again.
- Damage lands with the effect: a move whose blade or ball flies only waits for what is LEFT of its flight (a
  projectile that hits you itself is never made to wait a second time), at most 8 ticks, and a target within
  2.5 blocks is hit at once.
- Mercury Sphere: you can see again. Big balls like the barrier are drawn as a thin, gappy shell of small sprites
  instead of a solid mass of big ones, and from inside the shell is fainter still.
- Much less lag: the pixel-sprite cap is 2400 (was 6000), sprite-only effects emit about 40% less, new sprites
  thin out automatically the busier the screen gets, and far-away sprites and glows are skipped.

NEW IN 1.55.1
- Fix for playing with Essential: Essential 1.5.x could crash the game (ConcurrentModificationException in its
  GameProfileManager) when it read your skin while it was being updated. That read is now made safe - Essential
  just skips that one frame and tries again on the next. Does nothing if Essential is not installed.

NEW IN 1.54.0
- Every impact frame redone, the way anime draws them:
  - Negative frames: the world itself flips to a photo negative tinted with the grimoire's colour, so you still see
    who got hit and where - no more flat white screen. They alternate with ink frames (the grimoire's dark ink, not
    fully opaque) carrying its pattern.
  - Aimed at the hit: the spot that was struck is found on your screen and the speed lines, spikes, bolts, flames,
    cracks and cuts all converge on it or burst out of it, rushing inward as the frame plays.
  - Tapered, sharp lines (needle points, blade-shaped cuts, flame tongues) instead of flat bars; Asta's black frames
    get a red flash star on the hit, the finisher's huge slash cuts right through it, Equinox splits the screen at it.
  - Snaps in like a cut, lets go smoothly over its last tick, and is drawn under the HUD so your hotbar and health
    stay readable.

NEW IN 1.53.0
- Damage never lands before the move: a move's hit now waits until its slash, bolt, ball or gust has actually come out
  on the swing, and then for it to fly from where it was launched to the target - a far target is hit a moment later
  than a close one, exactly when the effect reaches it.
- The move sounds wait for the move the same way, so the swing, the sound, the effect and the hit happen together.
- New sounds from copyright-free (CC0) packs: sword slashes by StarNinjas; blasts, impacts, rushes, charges, flames,
  thunder and roars from Kenney's Sci-Fi Sounds. All mono (so they come from where the move is) and loudness-matched

NEW IN 1.47.0

    * every sprite is redrawn at double resolution (EPX pixel upscaling: smoother curves, still crisp pixels)

    * sprites no longer just fade - they dissolve in a 4-frame ordered dither, the classic pixel-art fade-out,

    beams        plain / double helix / pulse rings along it / crackling side-sparks

    orbs         plain / comet tail / orbiting sprites / pulsing shell
    explosions   plain / second shockwave / rising column / heavy debris
    blocks       plain / dust burst at the base / glint running along / shards off the tip
    lightning    plain / scorch ring / extra forks / static left hanging
    tornadoes    plain / debris circling the foot / crown ring / crackling flecks

NEW IN 1.40.0
- NEW: client effect settings (config/iron_spell_grimoire-client.toml, each player sets their own; the defaults are exactly
  the current look, and nothing here changes what a spell does):
  - particle_density (0-1): how many smoke / flame / spark / dust particles spells make.
  - effect_opacity (0.1-1): how solid the 3D effects look (slashes, walls, beams, domes, body auras).
  - screen_shake (0-2): screen shake strength, 0 = off.
  - impact_frames (true/false): the flashing anime impact frames on big hits.
- Performance (nothing looks or plays differently):
  - effect packets are encoded compactly: vectors and numbers that are zero are no longer sent. Lossless - the
    client gets exactly the same data, in far fewer bytes. (Network protocol bumped: server and client must both
    run this version.)
  - the staged-effect scheduler only touches what is due this tick instead of scanning every pending step.
  - spell damage no longer searches the whole spell registry on every hit (looked up once, then cached).
  - per-tick effect copies are matched against a short list of their own instead of every effect on screen, and a
    replaced copy is removed directly instead of re-scanning the list.
- Code cleanup: the helpers every grimoire's effect code had its own copy of (sound, mid, rightOf, flat, rotY, clip,
  enemies, closestOnSegment, aimed, alive) now live in one shared class. Only copies that were exactly identical
  were merged; the two classes with a different version of one keep their own, so behaviour is unchanged.
