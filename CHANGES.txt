Arcane Addon v1.12.1 - all changed files since v1.0.0

HOW TO USE: extract this zip into the top folder of your project (the one with build.gradle)
and let it overwrite. Then build as usual.

NEW IN 1.12.1 - POLISH / BUILD-SAFETY PASS
  - Fixed a compile error: DarkKatanaItem used Properties without importing it (now Item.Properties).
  - Devil Union now swaps the grimoire's spells one tick after the cast instead of during it, so Iron's spell bar
    never sees its contents change mid-cast (same for Release / the union ending).
  - Added the missing display name for grimoire_page.
  - Checked: every cross-class call resolves, no missing imports, no lambda captures of changing variables,
    every event handler class is registered, client-only code is never reached from the server,
    all 39 spells and 11 items have names / descriptions / icons / models, no vanilla particles or status effects.

NEW IN 1.12.0 - DEVIL UNION MOVESET
  When Devil Union starts, every spell in the Black Grimoire is swapped for its devil version (new name, icon and move);
  when it ends (timeout, Release, death, logout) the normal spells come back. Slots, levels and locks are kept.
  Devil moves use BOTH swords: the main-hand sword strikes first, the other follows.
    Summon        -> Devil Twin Swap: every swap strike = Slayer whirlwind + Dweller volley together
    Piercing Dash -> Devil Rush: faster, longer, the ember trail bursts behind you
    Zetten        -> Devil Zetten: Twin Cleave (main sword, ember crater, then the other sword)
    Sky Splitter  -> Devil Sky Splitter: Twin Rift (two rifts, one per sword)
    Anti Magic    -> Anti-Magic Domain (16-block domain, cuts magic users; Slayer dome / Dweller 8 crescents)
    Ki Sense      -> Devil's Eye (48 blocks, 20 s)
    Instinct Guard-> Devil Instinct (3 s, counters every attack, +50%)
    Devil Union   -> Devil Union: Release (end it in one blast, up to 3x damage; Slayer meteor / Dweller vortex)
    Dweller Slash -> Devil Dweller: Triple Slash (+ sweeping cut if the Slayer is held too)
    Borrowed Slash-> Devil Borrowed: Four Elements (fire, ice, lightning, wind in a row)
  Devil icons: the originals re-forged in ember with a crown of black flame.
  NEW FILES: server/DevilBook.java, server/DevilFx.java, spells/DevilSpell.java, 10 devil spell icons
  CHANGED:   AstaFx, SummonQueue, DashQueue, SummonGreatswordSpell, PiercingDashSpell, GrimoireSpell, AddonSpellRegistry, en_us.json

NEW IN 1.11.0 - REAL DEMON WINGS
  The devil form's wings are rebuilt like real bat-style demon wings:
    - an arm (shoulder -> elbow -> wrist) that rises so the wrist sits up high, with a bone-white hooked claw at the wrist
    - four long finger bones of two joints each, fanning from the wrist (outward-up to down)
    - a leathery membrane stretched between the fingers with a scalloped trailing edge that sags between the tips,
      plus the last section running down to the waist; faint veins and a glowing ember edge
    - real wing motion: the arm swings from the shoulder, the elbow flexes a beat later, the fingers spread on the
      downstroke and close on the upstroke; folding tucks the fingers down along the arm; gliding spreads it flat
  Same movement states as before (standing / running / jumping / gliding / crouching), ~25% larger.
  CHANGED: client/FxRenderer.java

NEW IN 1.10.0 - DEVIL FORM, MORE ALIVE
  Wings: 5 blades per side, each bent in 3 joints; the flap is a wave travelling shoulder -> tip (tips lag behind).
    They react to movement: slow beat standing, swept back running, hard flaps in the air, spread flat to glide
    when falling, folded in when crouching. They burst open with an overshoot and a few big beats on transforming.
    Ember trails follow the wingtips and a glow pulse runs out along every blade.
  Crown: 10 black-flame tongues with 3 taller pulsing ones, leaning back as you run.
  Mantle: rippling shadow streams that get blown back by movement.
  Sigil: a slowly turning ember circle + star under your feet while on the ground.
  Burst: an ember shockwave the moment the form begins.
  CHANGED: client/FxRenderer.java

NEW IN 1.9.0 - DEVIL UNION FORM (original design)
  While Devil Union is active (50 s) the player is drawn with a devil form over their own skin:
    - shadow wings: 4 ember-edged blades per side with a dark membrane, unfolding over 0.75 s and beating slowly
    - a crown of black flame flickering above the head
    - a mantle of shadow streaming down the back
  It fades out over 1 s when the union ends (also on death / logout), and players who walk into range later see it too.
  New transformation pose "devil_union_awaken": power gathers (arms crossed, hunched), then bursts out
  (arms flung wide, head back) as the wings unfold.
  New colour: ember (9). CHANGED: client/FxRenderer.java, server/AstaFx.java, spells/GrimoireSpell.java,
  player_animation/arcane_animations.json
