ARCANE ADDON - EFFECT LIBRARY (server/FxLib.java)

25 recipes x 8 colours = 200 effects. Built only from the addon's own 3D shapes (no vanilla particles).
Total: 200 effects.

Use by name:   FxLib.play("spike_ring_steel", level, pos, dir, 1.0f);
Use by parts:  FxLib.play(FxLib.Recipe.SPIKE_RING, FxLib.STEEL, level, pos, dir, 1.0f);
  pos = centre (or feet / ground point where noted), dir = facing for directional recipes, scale 1.0 ~ player-sized

Colours: crimson, dark, fire, ice, lightning, wind, steel, gold
